Google Photos Location Check

Badges

Open any Google Photos album. Each thumbnail whose photo carries no location gets a crossed-out map pin in its top left corner. Photos are read as their thumbnails come into view, so badges fill in while you scroll.

Grey is not the same answer as orange. Orange means Google told us the photo has no location. Grey means we could not get an answer, which usually means Google changed something and the extension needs an update.

Speed

Leave these alone unless something misbehaves. Lower them if Google starts refusing requests; the panel on the album page reports that under Copy diagnostics.

Remembered albums

Each answer is remembered on this computer, so an album you have already looked at shows its badges at once. Forget them if a photo's location changed and the badge did not.
